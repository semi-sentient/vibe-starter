Verified on macOS this session; Linux/Windows are covered by the CI matrix (3-OS green is observable once the branch is pushed) plus a human run of the runbook. Needs a second account or a deliberately-red PR to judge honestly, and an admin's own view will always look permissive.

## Notes

- `CHANGELOG.md` is written by `npm run release` (git-cliff, from conventional commits) — don't hand-edit it; just verify it cuts correctly (above). Its header block, everything above the first `## ` heading, **is** `cliff.toml`'s `[changelog] header` byte-for-byte; editing one without the other duplicates the header on the next release. `CHANGELOG.md` is Prettier-ignored for the same reason.
